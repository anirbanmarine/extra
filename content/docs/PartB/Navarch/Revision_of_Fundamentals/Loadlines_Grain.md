---
title: Volumetric heeling moments
type: docs
---
Grain carrying bulk carrier cannot heel more than **\(12\degree\)** or **deck edge** cannot immerse due to shift of grain.

\[ \lambda_0 = \frac{ \Tau_{assumed}}{SF \times \Delta}\] where \(\Tau \)= Assumed heeling moments due to shift of grain,SF = Stowage factor of the grain and \(\Delta\)= Displacement.
