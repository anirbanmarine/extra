### **A. SOLAS-90 Compliance (For Existing ROPAX Ships)**

#### **Table: Structural Modifications for SOLAS-90 Compliance**

| **No.** | **Modification** | **Purpose** | **Diagram Description** |
|---------|------------------|-------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | **Additional Watertight Bulkheads** | Divide car decks into smaller compartments to **limit flooding spread**. | Two vertical partitions dividing the car deck into three sections. |
| 2 | **Watertight Doors on Car Decks** | Prevent water spread between compartments. **Automatically close** in case of fire/flooding. | Doors with watertight seals at the boundaries of each car deck section. |
| 3 | **Increased Freeboard** | Reduce risk of **water ingress** on car decks. | Higher vertical distance from the waterline to the car deck. |
| 4 | **Fire-Resistant Divisions (A-60)** | Contain fires in **car decks and accommodation**. | Bulkheads with A-60 fire resistance rating separating car decks from other spaces. |

### **B. SOLAS-90+ (50) Compliance (Enhanced)**

#### **Table: Structural Modifications for SOLAS-90+ (50) Compliance**

| **No.** | **Modification** | **Purpose** | **Diagram Description** |
|---------|------------------|-------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | **Double Hull or Side Shells** | Improve **damage survivability** (e.g., side shells on car decks). | Outer hull and inner shell creating a double-layered structure around car decks. |
| 2 | **Automatic Sprinklers in Car Decks** | **Extinguish fires early** to prevent spread. | Sprinkler heads installed across the ceiling of car decks. |
| 3 | **Improved Ventilation Control** | **Fire dampers** to prevent **smoke/fire spread** through ducts. | Dampers in ventilation ducts that close automatically during a fire. |
| 4 | **Additional Lifeboats** | Ensure **evacuation from both sides** of the ship. | Lifeboats positioned on both port and starboard sides. |
| 5 | **Enhanced Drainage Systems** | **Remove water quickly** from car decks (scuppers + pumps). | Scuppers and pumps integrated into the car deck floor for rapid water removal. |

### **Summary of Key Alterations**

#### **SOLAS-90 Compliance**
- Focuses on **basic subdivision and fire resistance** to limit damage spread.
- Ensures **watertight integrity** and **fire containment** in existing ROPAX ships.

#### **SOLAS-90+ (50) Compliance**
- **Enhanced subdivision** (double hulls, side shells).
- **Active fire suppression** (automatic sprinklers, fire dampers).
- **Improved evacuation** (additional lifeboats, better drainage).
- **Higher damage stability** (survive flooding of two adjacent compartments).
