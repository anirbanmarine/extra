## Powering
BHP = Brake Horse Power,
SP = Shaft Power,
DP = Delivered Power,
TP = Thrust Power,
EP = Effective Power.

BHP>SP>DP>TP>EP.

Therefore to calculate the powering requirement of an Engine we must do a back calculation coming from shape and size of ship intended to be built.

Now 	\[ EP = R_t \times V_s\]
where   \[R_t=Total Resistance \]
 	\[V_s= Intended Service speed\]
## Efficiency 
Hull Efficiency \[ H_s = \frac {EP}{TP}\]
Propeller Efficiency \[P_e= \frac{TP}{DP}\]
Propulsive Efficiency \[ PS_e=\frac {EP}{SP}\]
