## Resistance
\(R_t\) = Total resistance
\(R_f\) = frictional resistance
\(R_r\) = Residual resistance (Wave and eddy making resistance)

\[R_t= R_r+ R_f\]

## Frictional Resistance
Where
\(f\)= coefficient of friction
\(A\)= wetted surface area
\(V\)= ship's speed
\(n\)= coefficient dependent on roughness of hull

\[f = \frac{0.441}{L_s^0.0088}\]

\[R_f = f \times A \times V^n \]

## Froude's Speed Length Law

Velocity of ship = \(V_s\)
Velocity of model = \(V_m\)
Length of ship = \(L_s\)
Length of model = \(L_m\)

\[\frac {V_s}{\sqrt {L_s}} = \frac{V_m}{\sqrt {L_m}}]

# Residual Resistance 

\(R_{rs}\)= Residual resistance of ship
\(R_{rm}\)= Residual resistance of model
\(L_s\)= Length of Ship
\(L_m\)= Length of model

\[\frac{R_{rs}}{R_{rm}}{{\frac{L_s}{L_m}}^3}\]

# Wetted Surface Area

\[\frac{WSA_{ship}}{WSA_{model}}={\frac{L_s}{L_m}}^3\]
