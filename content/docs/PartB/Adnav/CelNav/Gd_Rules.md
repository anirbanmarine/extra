1. When the body is on Observer's Rational Horizon ,side \(ZX = 90^ \circ \).
2. At Meridian Passage of Sun LAT is always 1200h.
3. Earth's radius is 6350km.
4. When the body is at Observer's Prime Vertical \( \angle Z = 90^\circ \)
5. Amplitude takes name from from Declination. A body will rise from North of Oberver's Prime vertical only if her declination  is north and vice versa.
6. \[Sin (Amp) = \frac{Sin(Decl)}{Cos(Lat)}\]
7. For a body to cross Prime vertical L and D are of same name and D < L.
8. Sidereal day is the duration of time between two successive crossing of a distant star over same meridian = 24 hrs.
9. Solar day is the duration between two successive transits of the centre of the sun over same meridian = 23 hrs 56 mins 4.1 secs.
10. Lunar day is the duration between two successive transits of the center of the moon over the same meridian = 24 hrs 50 mins approx.
11. Synodic period is the duration between two successive conjunctions of a distant fixed star and the body in Question which can be sun, moon or planets.
12. Sidereal period of moon is the time taken by the Moon to go around the Earth once = 27 d 43 m 11.5 s.
13. Lunation is the time between two successive conjunctions between Sun ,Moon and the Earth.
14. \[ \frac{1}{S_y}= \frac{1}{S_D}=\frac{1}{Y_E}\] where \(Y_E\) is the sidereal period of revolution of earth.
15. Rate of change of meridian by Sun is \(15^\circ / hour \)
16. Rate of change of meridian of Aries is \(15 \degree 2.46'\)
17. 'v' correction of Moon = Actual hourly rate of change of hour angle of moon - \(14 \degree 19'\).
18. 'v' of planet = Actual hourly rate of change of meridian - \(15\degree\)
19. **Ephemeris** of heavenly bodies given in the Nautical almanac is given in the **Equinoctial System of Co-Ordinates**.
20. \[Solar Time = \frac { Solar Hour Angle}{15\degree} \pm 12 hours \] Related to Sun - True Sun ,Mean Sun GMT,LMT,GAT and LAT.
21. Sidereal time = Star Time \[ Local Sidereal time =\frac{ \text(Local Hour angle)}{15 \degree} \]
22. \[ LAT = \frac{LHA True Sun}{15\degree} \pm 12 hours \]
23. \[LMT = \frac{LHA Mean Sun}{15 \degree} \pm 12 hours \]
24. Equation of Time is the angle contained between meridians of  True Sun and Mean Sun at the celestial pole.
25. \[ EOT = SHAMA - SHATS\]\[ EOT = GHAMA -GHATS\]\[ EOT = LHAMS - LHATS\] \[ EOT = GMT - GAT\]\[EOT = LMT-LAT\]\[EOT = RATS -RAMS\]
26. \[ e = \frac { \text(center to focus)}{\text(half major axis)}\] where e is eccenticity.
27. \[ e^2 = 1-\frac{ b^2}{a^2}\]
28. perigee and apogee calculations \(SA = a(1-e)\),\(SA'= a(1+e)\).
29. Rate of change of Altitude :\[dh = dz \times Sec (Lat) \times Cosec(Azimuth)\].
30. Rate of change of Azimuth: \[ dA = dh \times Cos(Declination) \times Sec (Altitude) \times Cos(\angle PXZ)\]. \(\angle PXZ = parallectic angle\)
31. At Meridian Passage
    - at Upper Meridian Passage  \(MZD = (L \sim D)\)
    - at Lower Meridian Passage \(MZD = 180-(L+D)\)
    - \[dA = 15' \times \frac {Cos(Declination)}{Sin (L \sim D)}\] per minute of time.
