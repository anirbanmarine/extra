Summary table covering all ship's magnetic compass coefficients—including **\(\lambda\) (lambda)** and **\(\mu\) (mu)**—detailing their names, deviation types, causes, formulas, course and latitude effects, and methods of correction.

### Magnetic Compass Coefficients & Parameters Table

| Coefficient / Parameter | Type of Deviation | How Caused | How Found / Formula | Effect of Course | Effect of Latitude | How Corrected / Adjusted |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Coefficient A** | Constant deviation (same magnitude & sign on all headings). | Asymmetrical placement of compass off centerline, lubber line misalignment, observation errors, or transient effects during rapid swinging (Gaussin/Retentive error). | \(A = \frac{\delta_N + \delta_{NE} + \delta_E + \delta_{SE} + \delta_S + \delta_{SW} + \delta_W + \delta_{NW}}{8}\) (Mean of deviations on 8 main headings). | Unaffected by change of course (constant on all headings). | Independent of magnetic latitude. | Physical/mechanical alignment of compass bowl/lubber line, or allowed for mathematically. |
| **Coefficient B** | Semicircular deviation (maximum on E/W, nil on N/S). | Fore-and-aft magnetic forces: Permanent magnetism (\(P_B\)) from hard iron in hull, and Induced magnetism (\(i_B\)) in vertical soft iron (\(c\)-rod) induced by \(Z\). | \(B = \frac{\delta_E - \delta_W}{2}\) (Calculated from deviations on East and West headings). | Varies directly as \(\sin(\text{compass course})\): \(\delta_B = B \sin \theta\). | \(P_B \propto \frac{1}{H}\); \(i_B \propto \frac{Z}{H} = \tan(\text{dip})\). Total \(B\) changes with magnetic latitude. | \(P_B\) corrected by Fore-and-Aft permanent magnets in binnacle. \(i_B\) corrected by a vertical soft iron **Flinders Bar** placed fore or aft . |
| **Coefficient C** | Semicircular deviation (maximum on N/S, nil on E/W). | Athwartship magnetic forces: Permanent magnetism (\(P_C\)) from hard iron in hull, and Induced magnetism (\(i_C\)) in vertical soft iron (\(f\)-rod) induced by \(Z\). | \(C = \frac{\delta_N - \delta_S}{2}\) (Calculated from deviations on North and South headings). | Varies directly as \(\cos(\text{compass course})\): \(\delta_C = C \cos \theta\). | \(P_C \propto \frac{1}{H}\); \(i_C \propto \frac{Z}{H} = \tan(\text{dip})\). Total \(C\) changes with magnetic latitude. | \(P_C\) corrected by Athwartship permanent magnets in binnacle. \(i_C\) corrected by slewing Flinders Bar or athwartship Flinders bar . |
| **Coefficient D** | Quadrantal deviation (maximum on NE, SE, SW, NW; nil on N, E, S, W). | Induced magnetism in symmetrical horizontal soft iron: continuous athwartship beams (\(-e\) rod, \(+D\)) and continuous F&A girders (\(-a\) rod, \(-D\)) induced by \(H\). | \(D = \frac{\delta_{NE} - \delta_{SE} + \delta_{SW} - \delta_{NW}}{4}\). | Varies directly as \(\sin(2 \times \text{compass course})\): \(\delta_D = D \sin 2\theta\). | Independent of magnetic latitude (disturbing force and directive force \(H\) change in exact same proportion). | **Soft Iron Spheres** (divided athwartship soft iron) placed on brackets on either side of binnacle. |
| **Coefficient E** | Quadrantal deviation (maximum on N, E, S, W; nil on NE, SE, SW, NW). | Induced magnetism in unsymmetrical horizontal soft iron situated diagonally (\(45^\circ\)) to F&A line (\(b\) and \(d\) rods of same name). | \(E = \frac{\delta_N - \delta_E + \delta_S - \delta_W}{4}\). | Varies directly as \(\cos(2 \times \text{compass course})\): \(\delta_E = E \cos 2\theta\). | Independent of magnetic latitude. | Soft Iron Spheres placed diagonally at \(45^\circ\), or by **slewing** \(D\)-correcting spheres through angle \(M\) where \(\tan 2M = \frac{E}{D}\). |
| **Heeling Error (HE)** | Semicircular deviation when ship heels (maximum on N/S, nil on E/W). | Vertical forces (permanent vertical force \(R\), induced vertical \(k\)-rod, and athwartship \(e\)-rod) acting at an oblique angle when ship heels. | Difference in deviation between heeled and upright conditions, or assessed using Vertical Force Instrument (VFI) . | Varies directly as \(\cos(\text{compass course})\) and angle of heel \(i\). | Varies with latitude as \(\frac{Z}{H} = \tan(\text{dip})\) for induced parts and \(\frac{1}{H}\) for permanent part \(R\) (reappears with change of latitude) . | Vertical permanent magnets placed in **Heeling Error Bucket** below compass bowl inside binnacle . |
| **Lambda (\(\lambda\))** | **Directive Force Ratio** (Not a deviation; mean horizontal directive force ratio) . | Attenuation of Earth's \(H\) by continuous steel structure (\(\lambda_1\)), restored/increased by divided soft iron spheres (\(\lambda_2\)) . | \(\lambda_1 = \frac{\text{Mean } H \text{ at compass}}{\text{Mean } H \text{ ashore}}\) (before spheres); \(\lambda_2 = \frac{\text{Mean } H \text{ at compass}}{\text{Mean } H \text{ ashore}}\) (after spheres) . | Mean ratio over all \(360^\circ\) courses (constant ratio for a given configuration) . | Virtually independent of latitude as force at compass and ashore vary proportionally with \(H\) . | Soft Iron Spheres increase \(\lambda_1\) to \(\lambda_2\) (\(\approx 0.85 - 0.90\)). \(\lambda_2\) ("Ship's Multiplier") is used to set VFI for HE correction (\(n_1 = n \times \lambda_2\)) . |
| **Mu (\(\mu\))** | **Vertical Force Ratio** (Not a deviation; mean vertical force ratio) . | Screening and vertical magnetization of ship's hull and structure under Earth's vertical field \(Z\) . | \(\mu_1 = \frac{\text{Mean } Z \text{ at compass}}{\text{Mean } Z \text{ ashore}} = \frac{\text{VFI scale reading on board}}{\text{VFI scale reading ashore}}\) (before HE correction) . | Mean ratio over all \(360^\circ\) courses (constant ratio for a given configuration) . | Varies directly with Earth's vertical force \(Z\) across latitudes . | Adjusted using vertical HE corrector magnets until \(\mu_2 = \lambda_2\) at a well-corrected compass . |
